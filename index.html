Three rules to remember

Pentatonic = drop the 4 and 7Major scale 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 becomes 1 2 3 5 6. Minor pentatonic uses the same five notes from the minor root: 1 ♭3 4 5 ♭7.
Minor = start on the 6The relative minor uses the notes of the major scale starting on its 6th note. G major and E minor share every note.
Modes = start on another noteStart the major scale on its 2nd note and you get Dorian, 3rd Phrygian, 4th Lydian, 5th Mixolydian, 6th Aeolian, 7th Locrian.

The five CAGED positions

Each position is named after the open chord shape hidden inside it. Going up the neck the order is always C, A, G, E, D, and then it repeats.

Mode overview

From brightest to darkest: Lydian, Ionian, Mixolydian, Dorian, Aeolian, Phrygian, Locrian.
